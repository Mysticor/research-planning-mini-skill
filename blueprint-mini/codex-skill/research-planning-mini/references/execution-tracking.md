# Research execution and actual evidence
After the approved frozen blueprint, keep research-planning active: receive data,
track experiments/results/evidence/risks and next actions; log failures and changes.
Reuse nature-experiment-log for structured local logs from supplied text/images/
transcripts. Do not activate Feishu/Obsidian or remote writes unless requested.
Raw attachments remain intact; uncertain fields remain uncertain.

Each run records E/run ID, timestamps, config/version/seed, raw input/output,
analysis code and conclusion boundary. Maintain results_facts.csv with actual
values, units/n/CI/p (or justified N/A), repeats/seeds, source and analysis hashes.
Rebuild claim_registry.yaml from actual results, not planned hypotheses: claim ID,
importance, supporting experiments/result IDs/literature, evidence strength,
alternatives, boundaries, allowed wording, forbidden overclaim and status
SUPPORTED/PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED/UNSUPPORTED/CONTRADICTED/UNRESOLVED.
Central positive claims without direct actual evidence block handoff. Negative
findings may support bounded null/failed-hypothesis claims; retain all other states.

Append decision_log, deviation_log and failed_experiments. Never erase negative
results, cherry-pick exclusions or relabel post-hoc as planned. Build limitations
by data/method/measurement/statistics/generalization/engineering/causal constraints
and known failures. RESEARCH_DOSSIER_FINAL mirrors what actually happened and
includes failed hypotheses, deviations, actual novelty and reproducibility limits.
