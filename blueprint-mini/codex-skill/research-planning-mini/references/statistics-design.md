# Planned and actual statistics
Use nature-statistics in audit/design mode, stop before its manuscript prose step.
Predefine primary/secondary metrics, independent experimental unit, sample size
justification, repeats, seed policy, uncertainty/CI, tests/assumptions, effect size,
multiple comparisons, outliers/exclusions, missingness and significance threshold
where applicable. Unsupported choices are AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED, not invented.
For review/simulation, explicitly justify nonapplicable inference instead of
inventing p values. Avoid pseudoreplication and conflating runs with independent
subjects/sites. Power assumptions must cite evidence or remain assumptions.
Maintain PLANNED_ANALYSIS vs EXPLORATORY/POST_HOC; record outcome knowledge at
changes. Actual sheets state what was done, including deviations, not intended
analyses. Numerical facts preserve n, unit, repeats, seeds, uncertainty and source.
