# Canon, evidence and argument
Reuse nature-proposal-writer foundation-files, scope and evaluation designs through
adapters.md. Build research_canon.md, evidence_table.csv, argument_map.md and a
scope contract. Do NOT run its compose/revise/export-to-proposal workflow.

Canon entries: id, proposition, source type, source/locator, date, status,
contrary evidence, implication, allowed/forbidden interpretations.
Statuses: ESTABLISHED, SUPPORTED, DISPUTED, UNRESOLVED, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN,
TO_VERIFY, BLOCKER. Plausible inference remains inference. Conflicting findings
remain visible. Evidence table maps claim→source→strength→risk→status; argument map
links known→unknown→RQ→discriminating test, with alternatives and counterarguments.

A reviewer may recommend narrowing or abandoning a claim. Keep its history and
negative evidence even when removing it from the active scientific argument.
