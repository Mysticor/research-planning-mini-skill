# Acquire evidence before opportunities disappear
Plan by E ID, claim/RQ and acquisition time, not final figure number. Save raw,
processed and source data; original photos/videos/screenshots/sensor logs; time,
calibration, configuration, environment/field conditions; predictions,
segmentation/detection/tracks; checkpoints, training/test logs, code commit,
software versions/seeds; failed/abnormal samples, negative results and field notes.
For setups request overview, sensor mounting, camera viewpoint, device/specimen
and representative conditions while access still exists.

Inventory every asset: id, type, relative path, source, experiment ID, date,
provenance, what shown, processing history, publication permission, quality, notes,
SHA-256. Types: RAW_DATA, PROCESSED_DATA, RESULT_DATA, RAW_PHOTO,
EXPERIMENTAL_PHOTO, RAW_VIDEO, MODEL_OUTPUT, SEGMENTATION_OUTPUT, DETECTION_OUTPUT,
TRAJECTORY_OUTPUT, PRELIMINARY_PLOT, SCHEMATIC_SOURCE, LITERATURE_FIGURE,
ADAPTED_MATERIAL, OTHER. Permission is not implied by downloading a file.
Never use generative AI to alter real experimental photos or model result facts.
Table-ready source inventories and possible supplementary evidence remain options;
manuscript-writing/nature-figure decide final numbering, main/SI and layouts.


Prepare-not-decide also applies to owned raw/experimental photos, video, model and simulation outputs, benchmark/ablation CSV, sensor data and preliminary plots. Record asset_id, originals, what_it_shows, provenance, quality and potential value; never bind PHOTO_017 to a final manuscript Figure/panel or MAIN/SI. Stage 2 decides final placement.
