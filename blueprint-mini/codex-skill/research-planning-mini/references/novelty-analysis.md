# Gap and novelty
For each gap establish what is solved, what is not, why it matters, whether the
absence is a search limitation or true unmet need, closest papers, near-identical
methods and remaining contribution. Distinguish problem, method, theoretical,
data, measurement, experimental, engineering, scientific-insight and integration
novelty. First application in a setting is not automatically algorithmic novelty.
Maintain NOVELTY_THREAT_REGISTER with paper, similarity, threat level, overlapping
claim, surviving differentiation and action. Full-text blockers bound conclusions.

Before handoff search freeze date through current date, including newest preprints,
exact method and application terms, target journal and direct competitors. Do not
reuse an old novelty verdict. Link refreshed runs and compared new works; revise
claim wording, journal, differentiation or experiments when necessary. Reopen
research if central claims lose novelty/support, preserving earlier freeze.
